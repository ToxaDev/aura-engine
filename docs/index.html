The third way · Windows

Keep your player. Play it through AuraEngine.

AuraEngine installs a virtual ASIO device, Aura Player ASIO. Choose it as the output in foobar2000, JRiver, HQPlayer or Roon, press play — and the music goes through the same rack to your DAC, at the output rate, BIT-PERFECT if you switch it on.

  • One click to install. The driver goes in once — Windows asks for permission; update and removal are in the same view.
  • 44.1 to 768 kHz, two channels, 64- and 32-bit players. When the player changes rate, AuraEngine follows.
  • Switches by itself. The I view of the list listens as soon as a player starts and shows what comes in.
  • Spotify or any Windows program gets there too, through VB-Audio Hi-Fi Cable & ASIO Bridge (third-party, donationware).

The sound comes out about 2.5 s after the player, so a pause or a seek there is heard that much later.

The signature

Hybrid-Phase: no trade-off

Every FIR filter forces a choice. Linear phase keeps the stereo image coherent but pre-rings before every attack; minimum phase hits clean but warps timing. AuraEngine renders the track twice in full and switches between the two renders per transient.

  • Sustain — linear phase. The image stays put, nothing smears between the channels.
  • Attacks — minimum phase. The hit lands with no pre-ringing in front of it.
  • Inaudible switching. At zero crossings, a 1 ms smooth fade, one switch plan for both channels — they can never de-phase.
