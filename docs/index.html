AuraEngine — audiophile music player and FIR upsampler

An audiophile FIR upsampler and music player that converts and plays through one engine. A filter made for each track, up to 1536 kHz and DSD — on an ordinary CPU.

  • 2.0.1: Auto, a filter made for each track. The top of the Resolution slider makes an unwindowed sinc exactly as long as the playing track can use — no download. Two grids, Auto and Auto S; a small graph shows the difference. Experimental. How it works
  • Hybrid-Phase, reworked — it sounds new. The attack detector works on magnitude and keeps dense textures linear, hears the drum kit — kick, snare, hats — in the player and the converter alike; no αHP top-octave dip, the same level as without HP.
  • Aura EQ. Up to 24 bands, AutoEq headphone profiles, a balance and a speaker delay for an uneven room, heard at once — and the render equals the live sound bit for bit.
  • XTC, designed for a head. Crosstalk cancellation for speakers, built from the triangle you measure and checked on a dummy head. Cue Consistency in front of it gives a panned mix the time and level differences of a real place.

One engine, two ways

Convert it. Or just press play.

Convert

Renders hi-res files to keep and play anywhere — 24-bit FLAC checked against the 64-bit render, or DSF from DSD64 to DSD1024. No deadline: any filter, on any machine, one level per album.

The third way · Windows

Keep your player. Play it through AuraEngine.

AuraEngine installs a virtual ASIO device, Aura Player ASIO. Choose it as the output in foobar2000, JRiver, HQPlayer or Roon, press play — and the music goes through the same rack to your DAC, at the output rate, BIT-PERFECT if you switch it on.

  • One click to install. The driver goes in once — Windows asks for permission; update and removal are in the same view.
  • 44.1 to 768 kHz, two channels, 64- and 32-bit players. When the player changes rate, AuraEngine follows.
  • Switches by itself. The I view of the list listens as soon as a player starts and shows what comes in.
  • Spotify or any Windows program gets there too, through VB-Audio Hi-Fi Cable & ASIO Bridge (third-party, donationware).

The sound comes out about 2.5 s after the player, so a pause or a seek there is heard that much later.

New in 2.0 · Cue Consistency

A panned mix, a real place.

A studio pans a guitar left by making it louder on the left — that is all. A real guitar on your left also reaches your left ear first and your right ear in the shadow of your head. CUE gives each panned source those missing cues, for the angle the mix already gives it.

  • Reads the pan, band by band. A guitar, a voice and a piano each get their own angle, even playing at once. It does not invent a place: it reads the one that is there.
  • Time and head shadow. A true delay of the far ear, so the attack moves with it and no echoes appear, and the shadow of a rigid sphere for the highs.
  • Same loudness, nothing forced. L and R are rotated, not boosted. Mono and recordings with natural cues pass bit for bit; ambience is left alone.
  • Speakers with XTC, or headphones. In front of crosstalk cancellation it works where XTC works; for headphones, over the whole range.
  • The Cue window. The stage as CUE reads it: each source a column of its own sound, solid where it sounds now, in strips where the speakers alone would put it.
  • Hear the difference. A/B against the dry signal at the same level (key B), Stage width 30–90°, an (i) on every control and a tour of its own.

Player only, off by default · switch CUE on in the rack, then SET. It deliberately changes the sound, like XTC.

The signature

Hybrid-Phase: no trade-off

Every FIR filter forces a choice. Linear phase keeps the stereo image coherent but pre-rings before every attack; minimum phase hits clean but warps timing. AuraEngine renders the track twice in full and switches between the two renders per transient.

  • Sustain — linear phase. The image stays put, nothing smears between the channels.
  • Attacks — minimum phase. The hit lands with no pre-ringing in front of it.
  • Inaudible switching. At zero crossings, a 1 ms smooth fade, one switch plan for both channels — they can never de-phase.
  • New in 1.9.5 — it hears the drum kit. A steady kick can sit 14 dB under the bass inside a wall of synths. With the instruments pack, the player first lists the kit’s hits for the whole track — kicks, snare and hats from the separation networks — while the Hybrid-Phase unit shows the preparation; the converter takes the same lists. The unit’s live strip shows the onset envelope, the threshold and the switching as you listen.

Why AuraEngine

Studio-grade filters. Ordinary hardware.

Multistage upsampling runs a 30M-tap filter at ×8 on 27 ms of CPU per second of music on four efficiency cores — no graphics card needed, matching the single-stage output to −204 dBFS. Since 1.9.8 the long filter skips the silence before and after a track, bit for bit: the billion-tap filter at ×16 plays on 2.5–3 % of the CPU instead of 10–11 %, and Hybrid-Phase starts a new track in seconds. A filter for each track

Kaiser filters from 5K to 30M taps, designed offline in 128-bit, run in 64-bit — or Auto: an unwindowed sinc exactly as long as the track can use.

CPU first, GPU on Auto

Plays 30M taps on about a quarter of a core. When the processor cannot keep up, a discrete graphics card takes over by itself, without precision loss — never required.

DSD to DSD1024

DSF files from DSD64 to DSD1024; live DSD64–256 over DoP. Shaped noise above 48 kHz.

Verified, bit for bit

Every FLAC is checked against the render it came from. Bit-perfect output on all three systems.

Internet radio

Thousands of stations through the same rack, live. Plus an ASIO input for anything else on the PC.

Analyzer & scenes

A real-time analyzer from 5 Hz, and GLSL scenes that draw the music while it plays.
